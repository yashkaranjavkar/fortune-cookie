Before you start

This extension records which websites you use. Games and apps you choose can use this to personalise their content, so you don't have to fill in long questionnaires.

  • What it records: the website's host name (for example github.com), how often you visit it each day, and how long it's the tab in front of you.
  • What it never records: full addresses, page contents, searches, form entries, passwords, or anything in Incognito windows.
  • How long:
  • Where it's kept: only in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. You choose when to export it.
  • You're in control: pause at any time, remove any site, or delete everything from the dashboard. Removing the extension deletes all of it.
